## Use inside the paper or dissertation

Load the packages and shared design system in the manuscript preamble:

```tex
\usepackage{xcolor,graphicx,tikz,pgfplots}
\usetikzlibrary{positioning,arrows.meta,shapes.geometric,fit,backgrounds,calc}
\pgfplotsset{compat=1.18}
\input{../dissertation/latex_figures/logolens_style}
```

Then include an individual figure in the same way as `paper/main_accessibility.tex`:

```tex
\begin{figure*}[t]
  \centering
  \resizebox{\textwidth}{!}{%
    \input{../dissertation/latex_figures/fig_05_system_architecture}}
  \caption{LogoLens processing pipeline.}
  \label{fig:system-architecture}
\end{figure*}
```

PGFPlots figures 4 and 7 already manage their own page-relative widths and normally do not require `\resizebox`. Figures 8 and 9 require the `assets/` directory to remain beside their `.tex` source.
